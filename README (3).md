## Thesis

Asian Paints is still the clear #1 in Indian decorative paints (50%+ branded share, 1.7-lakh-outlet network) with a
fortress balance sheet: net cash, interest covered 26–43×, ROCE recovering toward 29%, record ₹7,088 Cr operating
cash flow in FY26. Volumes are back (+8.7% in FY26, +12.4% in Q4).

The problem is price. Birla Opus (₹10,000 Cr war-chest) and JSW / Akzo have ended the era of unchallenged pricing
power, so the historical 55–60× multiple is treated as a ceiling, not a base. At 48× FY27E EPS (a discount to Berger's
~51× for competitive intensity, partly offset by superior ROCE) fair value is ₹2,375 — about 10% below the market.
The DCF (₹772) is deliberately a cross-check: it shows how much future growth is already pre-paid in today's price.

## Key assumptions

| Input | Value | Why |

| Decorative volume growth | 8.5% → 6.5% | Management guides 8–10% for FY27; eases as competition persists |

| Revenue growth (derived) | 13% → 9% | (1 + volume) × (1 + price/mix) − 1 |
| EBITDA margin | 18.2% → 19.5% | Guided 18–20% band; VAM-VAE backward integration lifts margins slowly; below FY24 peak of 21.4% |
| Capex % revenue | 5% → 4% | VAM-VAE build-out completes FY27–28 |
| WACC / g | 11.5% / 4.5% | Beta 0.85; g = inflation + ~0.5% real |
| Target multiple | 48× FY27E EPS ₹49.5 | Bear 40× ₹1,979 / Bull 55× ₹2,721 |

## What would change the call

- **Up:** volume growth sustained above 10%, EBITDA margin crosses 20%, Birla Opus visibly retreats from discounting.
- **Down:** FY27 volume growth below 5%, margins break 17%, CCI investigation forces structural changes to the dealer network.
- **Buy trigger:** ~₹2,140 or below (≈10% discount to fair value), or 2–3 quarters of stable market share with 19%+ margins.
